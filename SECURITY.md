# Security policy

This repository contains documentation, not running software. There is no deployed service and no dependency surface to exploit.

## Reporting a concern

Two things are still worth reporting privately rather than in a public issue:

- **A reproduction that constitutes a working exploit** against a named third-party system, rather than a generic failure mode. Entries here should describe mechanisms, not hand anyone a weapon pointed at a specific product.
- **Confidential material that made it into an entry** — internal system details, customer data, or anything identifiable that a contributor should not have published.

Email m_waqar@live.com. Expect a reply within a few days.

## Scope note for contributors

Prompt-injection and agent-security failure modes belong in this catalog — that is the point of EVAL-04. Describe the class of attack and how to defend against it. Do not include payloads targeting a specific named product, and do not include anything you learned under an NDA.

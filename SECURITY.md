# Security policy

This repository holds documentation, not running software. There is no deployed service and no dependency surface to attack.

## Reporting a concern

Two things are still worth reporting privately rather than opening a public issue:

- **A reproduction that amounts to a working exploit** against a named third-party system, as opposed to a general failure mode. Entries here describe mechanisms. They should not hand anyone a weapon pointed at a specific product.
- **Confidential material that made it into an entry.** Internal system details, customer data, or anything identifiable that a contributor should not have published.

Email m_waqar@live.com. Expect a reply within a few days.

## Scope note for contributors

Prompt injection and agent-security failures belong in this catalog. That is the point of EVAL-04. Describe the class of attack and how to defend against it. Do not include payloads targeting a specific named product, and do not include anything you learned under an NDA.
